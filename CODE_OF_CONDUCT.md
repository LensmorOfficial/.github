# Code of Conduct

Lensmor open-source projects should stay professional, specific, and useful.

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat other contributors with respect.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing confidential or proprietary information.

If you see behavior that violates this code, contact [hello@lensmor.com](mailto:hello@lensmor.com). Lensmor may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project healthy.
