# Security Policy

We take the security of Lensmor and our open source projects seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**

Instead, email **security@lensmor.com** (or **hello@lensmor.com** if that
bounces) with:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The affected repository, URL, or component

We will acknowledge your report within **5 business days** and aim to provide a
remediation timeline after our initial assessment. Please give us a reasonable
opportunity to address the issue before any public disclosure.

## Scope

This policy covers the repositories under the
[LensmorOfficial](https://github.com/LensmorOfficial) organization and the
Lensmor platform at [lensmor.com](https://www.lensmor.com/). Vulnerabilities in
third-party dependencies should be reported to their respective maintainers.

Thank you for helping keep Lensmor and our community safe.
