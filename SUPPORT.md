For our free tools, datasets, templates, and lists:

- **Found a bug or have a question?** Open an issue in the relevant repository.
- **Want to suggest a resource or fix data?** See
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and open a pull request.
- **General feedback or ideas?** Use the
  [feedback form](https://github.com/LensmorOfficial/.github/issues/new?template=general-feedback.yml).
